# Business Insights

## Portfolio Scale

The dashboard contains 7,299 policies and provides a consolidated view of premium, maturity, protection, and sales performance.

## Premium Collection

The dashboard separates premium paid from premium payable.

This distinction helps users understand:

- How much premium has already been collected.
- How much premium remains payable.
- The level of future collection exposure.
- The overall progress of premium payments.

## Product Contribution

The portfolio includes Endowment, Universal, and Whole policy categories.

Comparing premium contribution across policy types helps identify the products generating the highest business volume.

## Geographic Performance

The state-level analysis identifies locations contributing the highest premium values.

This information can support:

- Regional sales planning.
- Targeted marketing.
- Resource allocation.
- Market prioritization.

## Occupation-Based Segmentation

Occupation-based analysis provides an additional perspective for understanding customer segments.

It can support:

- Customer profiling.
- Product targeting.
- Marketing campaign design.
- Portfolio segmentation.
- Underwriting analysis.

## Investment and Maturity Analysis

The Investment Value vs. Maturity Value page compares the amount invested with the expected maturity value.

The ROI line adds a return-oriented perspective and helps identify changes across policy years.

## Protection Value

The Annual Premium vs. Protection Value page evaluates the relationship between the customer’s annual contribution and the protection provided by the policy.

This helps users compare financial contribution with insurance coverage.

## Sales Hierarchy

The Sales Hierarchy page connects financial outcomes to organizational responsibility.

Users can analyze performance from the zonal level down to the regional, agent, policy-type, and policy-name levels.

## Premium Duration

The Premium Analysis page provides visibility into premium paid, premium payable, maturity amount, and ROI across different policy durations.

This supports future collection planning and policy-duration analysis.

## Interpretation Note

The dashboard identifies patterns and relationships in the available data. Additional business context, policy definitions, actuarial assumptions, and data validation would be required before using the results for production decisions.
